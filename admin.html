Products

Koi bhi change karke Save dabayein — store par foran live ho jayega. Har cheez yahin se editable hai: naam, price, category, stock, featured, rating + reviews, har product ki apni delivery fee (0 = FREE delivery), aur card par nazar aane wala 🏷️ discount % badge (khali = price se auto). Product photo ke liye apni image file upload karein ya image URL paste karein.

Categories

Categories store ke nav, tiles aur footer mein nazar aati hain. Visible off karne se category wahan se gayab ho jati hai, lekin us ke products All Products mein rehte hain. Category delete karne se products delete NAHI hote — un par category ka text waisa hi rehta hai.
